# Roles and Permissions

This document defines the user roles supported by the system,
their responsibilities, and their access scope.

The system follows a role-based access control (RBAC) model
with support for multiple roles per user.

---

## 1. Super Admin

### Description
System-level administrator with full access.
Typically limited to founders or technical administrators.

### Permissions
- Full access to all system data and features
- Configure global business rules and system settings
- Manage integrations with external platforms
- Manage contracts and commission policies
- View and modify all financial and operational data

### Restrictions
- Not intended for daily operational use

---

## 2. Operator Admin

### Description
Primary operational role for Elemnt Rentals staff.
Responsible for day-to-day management of properties and reservations.

### Permissions
- Create, modify, and manage reservations
- Register check-in and check-out
- Manage properties
- Register operational expenses
- Create charges to guests (damages, penalties, extra services)
- View payments and payment statuses
- Access operational and financial reports

### Restrictions
- Cannot modify global system rules
- Cannot change commission percentages or contract policies

---

## 3. Finance / Accounting

### Description
Financial role responsible for validating payments and financial reporting.

This role may coexist with other roles (e.g. Operator Admin)
within the same user account.

### Permissions
- View all payments
- Validate payments (cash, transfers, manual confirmations)
- Export financial reports
- Review commissions and owner settlements

### Restrictions
- Cannot create or modify reservations
- Cannot manage properties or guests
- Cannot register operational expenses

---

## 4. Property Owner (Partner)

### Description
Property owners whose properties are managed by Elemnt Rentals.

### Permissions
- View their own properties
- View reservations associated with their properties
- View income generated by their properties
- View a detailed breakdown of operational expenses
- View charges related to damages or penalties
- Access and download financial reports and settlements

### Restrictions
- Read-only access
- Cannot modify properties, reservations, or financial data
- Cannot view data from other owners

---

## 5. Guest (Future Scope)

### Description
End customers who stay at the properties.

### Notes
- This role is not included in the MVP
- It may be implemented in future phases for:
  - Viewing reservations
  - Completing payments
  - Receiving charges

---

## 6. Multi-Role Users

- A single user account may have multiple roles assigned.
- Permissions are cumulative.
- This avoids the need to switch accounts for users
  performing both operational and financial tasks.

---

## 7. Access Control Principles

- Users can only access data required for their role(s)
- Property owners are strictly isolated from each other
- Financial data is protected based on role scope
- All sensitive actions should be auditable

---

## 8. AI and Automation Considerations

- AI agents operate under delegated role contexts
- AI actions must respect the same permission boundaries
- Reporting agents should operate under Finance or Owner contexts
