## Accepted Changes

### 1. Currency Localization (COP)

**Implementation**:
- Created `frontend/src/lib/currency.ts` with `formatCOP()` utility function
- Applied to PropertyCard and PropertyDetail components
- Format: `$ XXX.XX COP` with locale-based thousands separators

**Impact**: All prices throughout the application now display in Colombian Pesos (COP) instead of USD, eliminating currency confusion for users.

**Minor Note**: Displays decimals (`.00`) which is uncommon for COP but acceptable and non-blocking.
