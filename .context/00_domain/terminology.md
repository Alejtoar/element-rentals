# Terminology (Glossary)

* **Operator:** ELEMNT RENTALS S.A.S.
* **Mandate:** The legal agreement where the Owner allows the Operator to act on their behalf.
* **Depurated Income:** Gross revenue minus channel commissions, cleaning costs, and basic supplies.
* **Owner Payout:** The final 80% share of the Depurated Income sent to the Owner.
* **Charge:** An additional bill sent to a guest for damages or penalties (separate from the rental rate).
* **Check-in/Check-out:** The formal process of delivering and receiving the property.
* **No Show:** When a guest fails to arrive on the scheduled date without prior notice.
